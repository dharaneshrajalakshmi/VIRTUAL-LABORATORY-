Welcome to Virtual Laboratory

The Engineering Virtual Laboratory is an online learning platform designed for engineering students. It provides experiment details, objectives, procedures, equipment images and instructional videos.

Students can improve their practical knowledge and understand engineering concepts through digital learning.

Available Experiments

Experiment 1: Ohm's Law

Objective:

To verify Ohm's Law and calculate electrical resistance.

Procedure:

  • Connect the electrical circuit.
  • Apply different voltage values.
  • Measure the current.
  • Calculate resistance using V/I.

Experiment 2: Logic Gates

Objective:

To study the working of AND, OR and NOT logic gates.

Procedure:

  • Connect the logic gate circuit.
  • Apply different input combinations.
  • Observe the output.
  • Verify the truth table.

Experiment 3: DC Motor

Objective:

To understand the working principle of a DC motor.

Procedure:

  • Identify the motor terminals.
  • Connect the DC power supply.
  • Switch on the power.
  • Observe the motor rotation.
