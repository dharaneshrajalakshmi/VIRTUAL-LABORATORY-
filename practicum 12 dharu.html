Welcome to Virtual Laboratory

The Engineering Virtual Laboratory is an online learning platform designed for engineering students. It provides experiment information, objectives, procedures, equipment images and instructional videos. Students can understand practical engineering concepts through digital learning.

Engineering Experiments

Ohm's Law

Objective:

To verify Ohm's Law and calculate electrical resistance.

Procedure:

Connect the circuit, apply different voltage values, measure the current and calculate resistance using V/I.

Logic Gates

Objective:

To study AND, OR and NOT logic gates.

Procedure:

Connect the logic circuit, apply input combinations, observe outputs and verify the truth table.

DC Motor

Objective:

To understand the working principle of a DC motor.

Procedure:

Identify motor terminals, connect the DC supply, switch on the power and observe rotation.
