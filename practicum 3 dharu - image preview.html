Welcome to Virtual Laboratory

This virtual laboratory helps engineering students learn practical experiments through objectives, procedures, equipment images and videos.

Available Experiments

1. Ohm's Law

Objective: To verify the relationship between voltage, current and resistance.

Procedure: Connect the circuit, measure voltage and current, and verify V = IR.

2. Logic Gates

Objective: To study AND, OR and NOT gates.

Procedure: Apply different input combinations and observe the output truth table.

3. LED Circuit

Objective: To understand the working of an LED.

Procedure: Connect the LED with a suitable resistor and observe its operation.
