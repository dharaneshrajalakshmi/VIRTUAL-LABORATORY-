Welcome to our Engineering Virtual Laboratory. This platform allows students to understand engineering experiments through objectives, procedures, equipment images and instructional videos. Students can explore experiments and improve their practical knowledge through digital learning.

Available Experiments

Experiment 1: Ohm's Law

Study the relationship between voltage and current.

Objective:

To verify Ohm's Law and calculate resistance.

Procedure:

  1. Connect the circuit.
  2. Apply different voltages.
  3. Measure the current.
  4. Calculate resistance using V/I.

Experiment 2: Logic Gates

Understand the working of basic digital gates.

Objective:

To study AND, OR and NOT logic gates.

Procedure:

  1. Connect the logic gate circuit.
  2. Apply different input combinations.
  3. Observe the output.
  4. Verify the truth table.

Experiment 3: DC Motor

Study the construction and operation of a DC motor.

Objective:

To understand the working principle of a DC motor.

Procedure:

  1. Identify the motor terminals.
  2. Connect the DC supply.
  3. Switch on the power.
  4. Observe the motor rotation.
