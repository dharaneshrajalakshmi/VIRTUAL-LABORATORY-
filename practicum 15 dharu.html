Experiment Objectives

Ohm's Law Experiment

To verify the relationship between voltage, current and resistance using Ohm's Law.

Procedure

1. Connect the resistor to the power supply.

2. Measure the voltage using a voltmeter.

3. Measure the current using an ammeter.

4. Calculate resistance using V = IR.

5. Compare the experimental results.

Logic Gates Experiment

To understand the operation of basic digital logic gates.

Procedure

1. Select AND, OR and NOT gates.

2. Apply different input combinations.

3. Observe the output values.

4. Verify the truth tables.

DC Motor Experiment

To study the working principle of a DC motor.

Procedure

1. Connect the motor to the power supply.

2. Switch on the power supply.

3. Observe the direction of rotation.

4. Study the effect of voltage changes.
