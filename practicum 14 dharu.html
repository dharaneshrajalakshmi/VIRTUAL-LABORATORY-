Experiment Objectives and Procedures

1. Ohm's Law Experiment

Objective: To verify the relationship between voltage, current and resistance.

Procedure:

  • Connect the resistor to the power supply.
  • Measure the voltage using a voltmeter.
  • Measure the current using an ammeter.
  • Calculate resistance using V = IR.
  • Verify the experimental result.
2. Logic Gates Experiment

Objective: To understand basic digital logic gates.

Procedure:

  • Select AND, OR and NOT gates.
  • Apply different input combinations.
  • Observe the output values.
  • Verify the truth table.
3. DC Motor Experiment

Objective: To study the working principle of a DC motor.

Procedure:

  • Connect the DC motor to the supply.
  • Switch on the power supply.
  • Observe the direction of rotation.
  • Study the effect of voltage changes.
4. Kirchhoff's Law Experiment

Objective: To verify Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws.

Procedure:

  • Construct a simple electrical circuit.
  • Measure current in different branches.
  • Measure voltage across components.
  • Compare the measured values.
