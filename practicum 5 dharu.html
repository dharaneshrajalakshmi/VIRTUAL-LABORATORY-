Laboratory Objectives

  • Understand engineering concepts practically.
  • Develop technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Perform experiments in a virtual environment.
  • Understand the working of laboratory equipment.
  • Improve experimental learning and knowledge.

Experiment Procedure

  1. Select an experiment from the experiment list.
  2. Read the experiment objectives carefully.
  3. Identify the required laboratory equipment.
  4. Watch the instructional video.
  5. Follow the experiment steps.
  6. Record the observations and results.
